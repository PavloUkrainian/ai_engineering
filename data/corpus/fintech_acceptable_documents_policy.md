# FinTech KYC Operational Policy: Hierarchy of Acceptable Identification Documents

> **Document Type:** policy  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2023-09-01  

---

## 1. Primary Identification Documents (Tier 1)

Only government-issued identity documents featuring a high-resolution photograph and unambiguous legal identity are accepted as primary proof of identity: 1) Ukrainian biometric Passport for traveling abroad; 2) Ukrainian ID-card (plastic); 3) Ukrainian internal passport booklet (1994 series) with required 25/45 age photographs; 4) Foreign international biometric passport; 5) Permanent residence permit (Посвідка на постійне проживання).

## 2. Prohibited and Deprecated Documents

The following documents are NOT accepted as primary proof of identity: student cards, employee badges, military service booklets (for civilian commercial onboarding), driving licenses (except where specifically permitted as secondary proof), temporary residence certificates without biometric chips, and expired documents past their official grace periods.
