# ARCHIVED: FinTech Operational KYC Rules v1.0 (2019 Edition - DEPRECATED)

> **Document Type:** policy  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2019-04-01  

---

## 1. Archived Rule: Unverified Email Scans (SUPERSEDED)

WARNING: THIS POLICY IS OBSOLETE AND NO LONGER IN FORCE. In 2019, customers were permitted to submit non-certified photocopies or plain black-and-white JPEG images via email without dynamic liveness detection. This rule was completely revoked by NBU Resolution No. 65 in 2020.

## 2. Archived Rule: Monthly Turnover Ceilings (OBSOLETE)

The former simplified limit of 2,000 UAH per month without secondary document verification has been replaced by the tiered risk framework established in 2021. Any system referencing this 2019 document must issue a deprecation warning.
