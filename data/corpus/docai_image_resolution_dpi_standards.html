DocAI Computer Vision Standard: Image Resolution and Pre-processing Benchmarks

Type: technical_guide Date: 2023-05-14

1. Minimum DPI Thresholds for OCR Accuracy

Optical Character Recognition of identity documents requires an effective resolution of at least 300 DPI across the entire bounding box of the document. For reading microprinted security text (<= 0.25 mm) or fine guilloche lines, 600 DPI is required. Images below 150 DPI suffer an exponential character error rate (CER > 18%).

2. Dynamic Range and Color Depth

Captured frames must feature 24-bit RGB color depth. Grayscale or aggressive binarization must be avoided during initial ingestion to preserve watermark gradients and fluorescent fiber detection under color filtering.
