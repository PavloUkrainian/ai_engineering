# Biometric Security Guide: ISO/IEC 30107 Presentation Attack Detection (PAD)

> **Document Type:** standard  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2022-07-28  

---

## 1. Presentation Attack Instruments (PAI) Taxonomy

Presentation attack detection categorizes spoofing attempts into: Level 1 (print attacks on 2D paper, digital photo replay on smartphone displays); Level 2 (curved 3D paper masks, high-resolution video replay on 4K tablets); Level 3 (custom silicone masks, latex prosthetics, real-time AI deepfake video injection).

## 2. Passive vs Active Liveness Mechanisms

Passive liveness analyzes skin texture, micro-reflections, pulse photoplethysmography (rPPG), and depth from a single continuous stream without requiring user commands. Active liveness prompts the user to perform randomized actions (smile, turn head 15 degrees left, blink twice within 3 seconds) to thwart static replays.
