## 1. Historic Notarized Paper Submission

HISTORIC REFERENCE ONLY: Under the 2018 guidance, individuals residing outside regional administrative centers were required to dispatch physical paper copies certified by a local notary through postal mail. This procedure has been totally superseded by remote digital identification via Diia and BankID.
