# Technical Specification: Ukrainian Citizen ID Card (Passport Format 2015+)

> **Document Type:** standard  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2021-12-05  

---

## 1. Multi-Layer Polycarbonate Construction

The Ukrainian ID card is a single-piece multi-layer polycarbonate card compliant with ISO/IEC 7810 format ID-1. All text elements on the surface and embedded laser-engraved images are produced via Nd:YAG laser marking inside the core polycarbonate layer without ink ribbons.

## 2. Validity Lifespan by Age Group

For persons aged 14 to 18 years, the Ukrainian ID card is issued with a validity period of 4 years. For persons who have attained 18 years of age, the ID card is issued with a validity period of 10 years. Expired ID cards are legally invalid for opening bank accounts or undergoing KYC verification.
