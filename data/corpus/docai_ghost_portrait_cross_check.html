Fraud Detection Guide: Ghost Portrait and Secondary Image Cross-Verification

Type: technical_guide Date: 2023-08-11

1. Secondary Ghost Portrait Characteristics

Polycarbonate ID cards and passports incorporate a secondary, smaller 'ghost' portrait laser-engraved into a clear window or printed with UV-luminescent ink. The facial geometry and feature vectors of the primary portrait and the secondary ghost portrait must yield a cosine similarity >= 0.88.

2. Tamper Indicators in Portrait Swapping

Fraudulent document alteration commonly involves cutting out and replacing only the primary photo while leaving the ghost portrait intact. A cosine similarity divergence below 0.70 between primary and ghost portraits constitutes definitive evidence of physical document tampering.
