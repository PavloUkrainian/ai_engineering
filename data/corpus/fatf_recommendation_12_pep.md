# FATF Recommendation 12: Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) Lifecycle and Monitoring

> **Document Type:** standard  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2022-11-01  

---

## 1. Definition of Foreign and Domestic PEPs

Foreign PEPs are individuals who are or have been entrusted with prominent public functions by a foreign country (e.g. Heads of State, senior politicians, senior government or judicial officials, military officers). Domestic PEPs are individuals entrusted with prominent public functions domestically. Family members and close associates are subject to the same diligence tier.

## 2. Risk Mitigation Measures for PEP Relationships

For all foreign PEPs, financial institutions must implement proactive risk management systems to determine if a customer or UBO is a PEP, obtain senior management sign-off before account opening, establish the source of wealth and funds, and conduct enhanced ongoing transaction scrutiny.
