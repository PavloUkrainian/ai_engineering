Compliance Manual: KYC Onboarding Red Flags and Suspicious Activity Indicators

Type: policy Date: 2023-04-05

1. Typical Onboarding Anomaly Red Flags

Red flags that mandate manual escalation include: 1) Multiple onboarding attempts from the same device hardware ID using different identity names; 2) Virtual private network (VPN) or Tor exit nodes cloaking residential IP address; 3) IP address geographical location differing by more than 1,000 km from the declared residential city.

2. Synthetic Identity Patterns

Synthetic identity fraud combines genuine RNOCPP tax identifiers with fabricated names or stolen passport numbers. Automated cross-referencing between the demographic registry and tax authority databases is required to detect inconsistencies before granting credit or payment limits.
