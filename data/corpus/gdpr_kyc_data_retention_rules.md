# Legal Compliance: KYC Data Retention and Storage Limits under AML Law and GDPR

> **Document Type:** regulation  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2023-01-10  

---

## 1. Statutory Five-Year Retention Requirement

Under Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine on AML/CFT, all customer identification files, video verification session recordings, document scans, and transactional records must be stored securely for a minimum of 5 years following the formal termination of the business relationship.

## 2. Right to Erasure (GDPR Art. 17) Exceptions

The GDPR Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten') is statutorily overridden by AML mandatory compliance obligations. A customer cannot demand the immediate deletion of their KYC verification files prior to the expiration of the mandatory 5-year anti-money laundering statutory window.
