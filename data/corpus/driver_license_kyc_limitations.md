# Compliance Guideline: Ukrainian Driving License Limitations in FinTech Verification

> **Document Type:** policy  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2023-04-18  

---

## 1. Non-Citizenship Status of Driving Licenses

Under Article 5 of the Law of Ukraine on the Unified State Demographic Register, a driving license certifies special technical qualification to operate vehicles and confirms identity within the borders of Ukraine, but does NOT certify Ukrainian citizenship or tax residency status.

## 2. Restricted Applicability in Banking Onboarding

Financial institutions may accept a driving license strictly as secondary identification document or for simplified tier-1 identification with monthly turnover ceiling not exceeding 5,000 UAH. Full KYC onboarding requires a primary citizenship travel document (ID card or Passport).
