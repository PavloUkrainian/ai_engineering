# Document Verification Standard: Ukrainian Internal Passport (1994 Model Booklet)

> **Document Type:** standard  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2022-03-10  

---

## 1. Structure and Photo Insertion Milestones

The 1994 booklet passport consists of 16 stitched paper pages with blue security fibers. A new photograph of the holder must be glued into the passport upon attaining 25 and 45 years of age (pages 3 and 5 respectively). The holder has a statutory window of 30 calendar days to insert the photo; during martial law, this requirement is extended until 30 days after cessation of martial law.

## 2. Stamp and Watermark Validation

The dry relief seal of the State Migration Service (DMS) must visibly press into the corner of the photograph and the substrate paper. The paper must exhibit a continuous watermark depicting the National Emblem of Ukraine (Trident) when held against a transmitted light source.
