Integration Guide: BankID NBU OAuth2 Protocol and Scopes for FinTech

Type: technical_guide Date: 2023-06-25

1. Authorization Flow and Scopes

BankID NBU operates over OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect. The client application initiates an authorization request with scopes 'customer_identity', 'passport_data', and 'tax_number'. The identifying bank cryptographically signs the returned customer payload with an X.509 certificate.

2. Data Freshness and Re-verification Requirements

Identification data received via BankID NBU carries an issuance timestamp. Under NBU rules, customer data obtained via BankID is considered freshly verified for a maximum of 12 months, after which updated identity data must be requested.
