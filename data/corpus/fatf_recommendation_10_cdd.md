# FATF Recommendation 10: Customer Due Diligence and Beneficial Ownership Standards

> **Document Type:** standard  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2023-06-10  

---

## 1. Core Principles of Customer Due Diligence

Financial institutions must undertake CDD when: establishing business relations; carrying out occasional transactions above the designated threshold (USD/EUR 15,000); there is a suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing; or the institution has doubts about the veracity or adequacy of previously obtained customer identification data.

## 2. Identification and Verification of Beneficial Owners

Institutions must identify the ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) holding a direct or indirect ownership interest of 25% or more in a legal entity. Verification requires reliable, independently sourced documents, data or information. Reliance on self-declarations without supporting registry extracts is strictly prohibited.
