Fraud Detection Guide: Font Typographical Anomaly and Kerning Verification

Type: technical_guide Date: 2023-01-24

1. Strict Monospace Geometry in MRZ

Every character in an authentic ICAO MRZ conforms strictly to the OCR-B typeface standard with a fixed pitch of 2.54 mm (0.1 inch). Inter-character gaps and baseline alignment must remain perfectly uniform across the entire 30 or 44 character line.

2. Digit Splicing and Thickness Anomalies

Digital alteration (e.g. changing birth year from 1999 to 1989) inevitably introduces subtle stroke-width discrepancies, antialiasing halos, or baseline vertical misalignment exceeding 2 pixels. Deep Siamese networks compare candidate glyphs against an authentic template database.
