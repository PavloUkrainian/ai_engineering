DocAI Quality Assessment: Algorithmic Blur, Glare, and Shadow Filtering

Type: technical_guide Date: 2023-07-22

1. Blur Detection using Laplacian Variance

Document sharpness is evaluated by convolving the grayscale image with a standard 3x3 Laplacian kernel and calculating the variance of the response map. Images exhibiting a Laplacian variance score below 100.0 are classified as blurred and immediately rejected with an in-app prompt to stabilize the mobile camera.

2. Specular Glare Region Masking

Specular glare occurs when camera flash or ambient lighting reflects directly off glossy plastic laminate. Pixels with luminance Y > 250 in the YUV space across a contiguous area exceeding 1.5% of the MRZ or VIZ boundary mask trigger an automatic glare rejection.
