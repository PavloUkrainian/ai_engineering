# FinTech KYC Policy: Proof of Address (POA) and Secondary Document Standards

> **Document Type:** policy  
> **Effective Date / Version:** 2023-09-15  

---

## 1. Acceptable Proof of Address Documents

Proof of Address (POA) is required when registering accounts with transaction limits exceeding EUR 1,000/month. Acceptable documents include: 1) Utility bill (electricity, water, natural gas, fixed broadband internet); 2) Bank account statement displaying full residential address; 3) Official tax assessment or local council registration certificate (Витяг про місце проживання). Mobile telephone bills are categorically rejected.

## 2. Validity Period and Format Restrictions

All POA documents must be dated within 90 calendar days prior to the submission date. Documents must clearly display: customer's full legal name, full residential address, date of issue, and official company logo/stamp. Digital PDF statements downloaded directly from bank portals are permitted if cryptographically signed.
