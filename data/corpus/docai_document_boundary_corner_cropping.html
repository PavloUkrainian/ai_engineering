DocAI Computer Vision: Perspective Correction and Corner Quad Detection

Type: technical_guide Date: 2022-10-18

1. Quad Corner Detection and Aspect Ratio Bounds

Document boundary localization uses deep segmentation networks to predict four corner coordinates (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left). All four corners must lie within the camera viewport. The aspect ratio of the rectified quad must conform to ID-1 (1.586 +/- 0.05) or ID-3 (1.420 +/- 0.05).

2. Perspective Warp Transformation

A 3x3 homography matrix transforms the perspective-distorted quadrilateral into an orthogonal rectangular canvas of standard pixel dimensions (1012x638 pixels for ID-1). Truncated corners exceeding 15 pixels along any edge result in a fatal cropping defect code (ERR_DOC_CROPPED).
